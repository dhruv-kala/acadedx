# Acadedx

> The Next Generation AI Learning Platform

---

## About

Acadedx is an AI-powered learning platform developed by Digital Global Information Systems (DGIS).

Unlike traditional homework-solving applications, Acadedx focuses on helping students understand concepts through personalized explanations, guided learning, adaptive practice, and intelligent tutoring.

The platform is designed for students from Class 1 through College and Competitive Examinations.

---

# Vision

To become the world's most trusted AI learning companion, empowering every student with personalized education anytime and anywhere.

---

# Mission

Make quality education accessible, engaging, personalized, and affordable using Artificial Intelligence.
