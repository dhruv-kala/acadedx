# Purpose

This document defines the business model for Acadedx.

It explains how Acadedx creates value for its users, generates revenue, acquires customers, builds partnerships, and scales into a sustainable global education technology platform.

---

# Executive Summary

Acadedx is an AI-powered education platform that helps students learn through personalized tutoring, AI-assisted homework, adaptive practice, and performance analytics.

The platform follows a Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) model with subscription plans for individuals and institutions.

---

# Vision

Build one of the world's most trusted AI-powered learning platforms while making quality education accessible, affordable, and personalized.

# Pricing Strategy

Acadedx follows a Freemium model.

### Free Plan

- Basic homework help
- Limited quizzes
- Progress dashboard

---

### Premium Plan

- Unlimited AI assistance
- Advanced study planner
- AI-generated notes
- Unlimited quizzes
- Unlimited worksheets
- Detailed analytics

### School Plan
