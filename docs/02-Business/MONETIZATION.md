# Acadedx Monetization Strategy

**Document Version:** 1.0.0

**Status:** Draft

**Owner:** Product Management

**Last Updated:** July 2026

---

# Purpose

This document defines the monetization strategy for Acadedx.

The goal is to build a sustainable business while ensuring students have access to high-quality educational tools. Monetization decisions should always support the mission of improving learning outcomes.

---

# Monetization Principles

Acadedx follows these principles:

- Education first
- Transparent pricing
- No hidden charges
- Affordable subscriptions
- Value before payment
- Premium features should save time or provide advanced capabilities
- Core learning should remain accessible

---

# Business Model

Acadedx follows a **Freemium SaaS** model.

Users can begin learning for free and upgrade to premium plans when they need advanced features.

---

# Subscription Plans

## Free Plan

Target Users

- New students
- Casual learners
- Users exploring the platform

Included Features

- Account creation
- Limited AI Homework Solver
- Limited AI Tutor chats
- Basic quiz generation
- Basic notes generation
- Progress dashboard
- Study streak tracking

Limitations

- Daily AI usage limit
- Limited worksheet generation
- Limited OCR uploads
- Basic analytics

---

## Premium Student Plan

Target Users

- Regular students
- Exam preparation

Included Features

- Unlimited AI Homework Solver
- Unlimited AI Tutor
- Unlimited OCR
- Unlimited quizzes
- Unlimited worksheets
- Advanced notes
- Personalized study planner
- Learning recommendations
- Exam preparation tools
- Detailed analytics
- Priority support

---

## Family Plan

Target Users

Parents with multiple children.

Included Features

- Multiple student accounts
- Parent dashboard
- Family progress reports
- Shared subscription

---

## Teacher Plan

Target Users

Teachers and tutors.

Included Features

- Classroom management
- Homework assignment
- AI quiz generation
- Worksheet generation
- Student analytics
- Lesson planning tools

---

## School Plan

Target Users

Schools and coaching institutes.

Included Features

- Institution dashboard
- Student management
- Teacher management
- School analytics
- Administrative controls
- Bulk licensing
- Dedicated support

Premium plans may include a free trial for eligible new users.

Trial users have access to most premium features for a limited period.

Potential discounts include:

- Student offers
- School volume discounts

- Referral rewards

Schools and organizations may purchase licenses based on:

- Number of students

---

# Future Revenue Opportunities

Potential future revenue streams:

- AI API platform
- Content marketplace
- Certification programs
- Professional learning courses
- Partner integrations
- White-label licensing

---

# Monetization Rules

The platform must never:

- Sell student data
- Display intrusive advertisements during learning
- Lock essential account access behind payment
- Mislead users about pricing

Premium should provide additional value, not create unnecessary barriers.

---

# Subscription Management

Users should be able to:

- View current plan
- Upgrade
- Downgrade
- Cancel subscription
- View billing history
- Download invoices
- Update payment methods

---

# Key Metrics

Track:

- Free users
- Premium users
- Conversion rate
- Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)
- Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR)
- Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)
- Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
- Churn rate
- Average Revenue Per User (ARPU)

---

# Risks

Potential monetization risks:

- Low conversion rate
- High infrastructure costs
- Competitive pricing pressure
- Payment failures
- Subscription cancellations

Mitigation strategies should be reviewed quarterly.

---

# Success Criteria

The monetization strategy is successful when:

- Users perceive premium plans as valuable.
- Subscription revenue supports product growth.
- Conversion rates improve over time.
- Customer churn remains low.
- Revenue enables continuous investment in product innovation.
