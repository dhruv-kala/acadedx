# Acadedx Pricing Strategy

**Document Version:** 1.0.0

**Status:** Draft

**Owner:** Product Management

**Last Updated:** July 2026

---

# Purpose

This document defines the pricing strategy for Acadedx. It outlines subscription plans, pricing principles, billing rules, regional considerations, promotional strategies, and future pricing evolution.

The goal is to balance affordability, accessibility, and long-term business sustainability.

---

# Pricing Principles

Acadedx pricing is guided by the following principles:

- Affordable for students
- Transparent and simple
- Flexible plans
- Fair value for premium features
- Regional purchasing power consideration
- Sustainable business growth

---

# Billing Cycles

Supported billing frequencies:

- Monthly
- Quarterly
- Annual

Annual subscriptions should provide approximately 25–30% savings compared to monthly pricing.

---

# Regional Pricing

Pricing should adapt to:

- Country
- Currency
- Local purchasing power
- Taxes
- Educational market conditions

The pricing engine should support country-specific pricing without requiring application code changes.

---

# Payment Methods

Supported methods:

- UPI
- Credit Cards
- Debit Cards
- Net Banking
- Digital Wallets
- International Cards

Future:

- Apple Pay
- Google Pay
- School Purchase Orders

---

# Discounts

Available discount types:

- Annual Plan Discount
- Student Campaigns
- Referral Rewards
- Promotional Coupons
- School Bulk Licensing
- Seasonal Offers

---

# Upgrade Policy

Users may upgrade at any time.

The remaining value of the current plan should be prorated toward the new subscription where applicable.

---

# Downgrade Policy

Users may downgrade after the current billing cycle ends.

Premium features remain available until the subscription expires.

---

# Cancellation Policy

Users may cancel subscriptions at any time.

Access to premium features continues until the end of the active billing period.

---

# Refund Policy

Refunds are handled according to applicable laws and platform policies.

General guidelines:

- Duplicate payments are refunded.
- Failed purchases are reversed.
- Subscription refunds are evaluated according to payment provider policies.

---

# Trial Policy

Eligible new users may receive a limited-time Premium trial.

Trial duration and eligibility rules should be configurable without code changes.

---

# Taxes

Applicable taxes (such as GST or VAT) should be calculated automatically based on the user's billing country.

Invoices must include required tax information.

---

# Invoice Requirements

Invoices should include:

- Invoice Number
- Customer Name
- Billing Address
- Tax Details
- Subscription Plan
- Billing Period
- Payment Method
- Total Amount

Invoices should be downloadable in PDF format.

---

# Pricing Configuration

Pricing values should **not** be hardcoded.

The platform should support configurable pricing stored in a secure backend or administration panel.

Administrators should be able to:

- Update prices
- Create promotions
- Configure discounts
- Launch regional pricing
- Enable or disable plans

without requiring a software deployment.

---

# Key Pricing Metrics

Track:

- Free-to-Premium Conversion Rate
- Average Revenue Per User (ARPU)
- Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)
- Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR)
- Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)
- Churn Rate
- Trial Conversion Rate
- Coupon Usage
- Plan Distribution

---

# Risks

Potential pricing risks include:

- Low conversion rates
- High cancellation rates
- Competitive pricing pressure
- Currency fluctuations
- Payment gateway failures

Pricing should be reviewed quarterly based on business performance and customer feedback.

---

# Success Criteria

The pricing strategy is successful when:

- Students perceive premium plans as valuable.
- Revenue supports sustainable product development.
- Conversion rates improve over time.
- Churn remains below target thresholds.
- Educational institutions adopt paid plans.
